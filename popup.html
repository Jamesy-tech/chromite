Connect to your Chromecast device On your screen you will see an icon at the top right of your screen with three dots. Click on that and scroll down to "Cast, save and share", then proceed to cast your device. Please find your TV/device that you are connecting to and select it. Once you are done, press Next.
Connect to your Chromecast device Next, make sure your Chromecast device is powered on and ready to cast. On that same cast menu you were on before, locate your device name and select it. Some Chromecasts may support an "Access Code" that is shown on the screen and you may use it if you like.
Troubleshooting Not connecting?
  • Is your Chromecast device turned on and ready?
  • Are you connected to the same Internet/WiFi as the Chromecast you are connecting to?
  • Is your TV's source on the Chromecast's HDMI source?
  • Are you finding your Chromecast on a list or using an Access Code? (both should work, we suggest trying both ways)
Try and test different methods. Some may work better than others.
